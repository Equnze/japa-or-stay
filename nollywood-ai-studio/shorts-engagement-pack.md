# Shorts engagement pack: titles, end cards and pinned comments

Copy for all 15 Shorts on https://www.youtube.com/@japaor_stay, following [templates/engagement-rules.md](templates/engagement-rules.md). Stats as of 18 Nov 2026.

**What each entry has**

- **New title:** paste it into YouTube Studio → Content → the Short → Details. The hook stays first because it's what shows in the feed, and the question goes on the end. For the five biggest Shorts the change is small, so you don't throw away a title that's working.
- **End card:** the text for `tools/end-card.js`, for when you re-cut the Short (a Part 2, the TikTok/Reels version, or a re-upload). The skits already have their own A/B card, so for those use the `--trim` line instead.
- **Closing line:** a voiced line in character for the next version or Part.
- **Pinned comment:** post it from the channel account and pin it (⋮ → Pin). It matches the A/B options already in each description.

**Before you start:** make a public playlist called **Chinedu's Japa Story** with Parts 1–4 in order, then the standalone episodes. Shorts in a playlist show a link to it, and that's an easy route to more views and subscribers.

## Order to do it in

1. **Today:** create the playlist and pin the comments on the five most-watched Shorts first (First Day at a UK Job, Black Tax, He Japa to London and Mama Called, ₦800k, Tunde Stayed). That's where the viewers already are.
2. **Today:** fix the "Pigin" title and the empty description on G5zuEpOz2u0.
3. **This week:** update the other titles, a few a day; you don't need to change them all at once.
4. **For every new skit:** render it with no logo outro (or run `node tools/end-card.js in.mp4 out.mp4 --trim <outro seconds>`), and put the pinned comment up within an hour of posting.
5. **For Part 5:** open with "Una vote…" and the winning answer from the Part 4 comments.
