# Code of Conduct

Japa or Stay is for everyone, whether you japa'd, stayed, or are still deciding. We want this project to be a friendly place to take part.

## Our standards

Do:

- Be kind and patient, especially with first-time contributors.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect different opinions about leaving or staying.
- Accept a "no" on an idea gracefully.

Don't:

- Insult, harass or threaten anyone.
- Make tribal, religious, ethnic, gender or other discriminatory remarks.
- Share someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, troll or post sexual content.

## Where this applies

In the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and comments, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, report it to the maintainer by sending a direct message to @japaor_stay on social media, or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment. Reports are kept private.

The maintainer may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or block people who break these rules.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
